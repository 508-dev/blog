# Why Fully Decentralized AI Is Mostly a Myth

Compute centralizes due to physics and economics.

## GPU Constraint

| Factor | Reality |
|--------|---------|
| GPU Cost | High |
| Utilization | Requires central scheduling |
| Latency | Blockchain too slow |

---

## 🧩 Case Study: Decentralized Inference Marketplace

A startup attempted token-incentivized GPU nodes.
Result: inconsistent uptime, latency spikes, centralized fallback required.
